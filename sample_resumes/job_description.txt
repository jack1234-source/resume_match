Backend Software Engineer

We are looking for a Backend Software Engineer to design, build and maintain reliable backend services and APIs.

Required qualifications and skills:
- 4+ years of professional backend development experience.
- Strong hands-on experience with Java and Spring Boot.
- Design and development of REST APIs and microservices.
- Good SQL knowledge and experience with PostgreSQL.
- Working knowledge of Docker and AWS cloud services.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology or a related field.

Preferred: Redis, Kubernetes, CI/CD, Linux, Agile/Scrum and automated testing.

Responsibilities include writing maintainable code, reviewing pull requests, debugging production issues, collaborating with frontend and product teams, and improving service performance and reliability.
